D. EVIDENCE
-----------
CONFIRMED FACTS (verified in-memory from `SLUS_214.48`):
- Entry point: `0x00100008`, `_gp`: `0x0030FFF0`.
- Executable EE regions:
  - `.text`: `0x00100000 .. 0x00280E88` (`0x180E88` B)
  - `.gnu.linkonce.t.*` (1,449 sections): spanning `0x00280E88..0x00291230` and
    `0x002953B0..0x00295EB8` (split around `.vutext`!).
- NON-EE region that MUST BE EXCLUDED from EE function discovery:
  - `.vutext`: `0x00291230 .. 0x002953B0` (`0x4180` B, 44 VIF `MPG` packets).
- C++ vtables: 284 `.gnu.linkonce.d.*` sections (`0x002E0A80..0x002E52D0`).
  In GCC 2.96 MIPS ABI, each vtable contains 8-byte slots `(delta/offset, func_ptr)`
  or 4-byte function pointers — INSPECT the actual bytes of `_vt$8DogActor` and
  `_vt$9DogEntity` in `SLUS_214.48` to confirm exact GCC 2.96 vtable slot stride
  before parsing all 284 vtables!
- Direct calls: 4,025 unique `jal` targets, all within `0x00100248..0x00295918`.
- Indirect calls: 558 `jalr` sites; 297 non-`$ra` `jr` sites (switch tables).
- Sony SDK libraries present: `libkernl 3000`, `libcdvd 3000`, `libmc 3020`,
  `libmpeg 3000`, `libipu 3000`, `libdma 3000`, `libvu0 3000`, `libdbc 3020`,
  `libpad2 3020`, `libvib 3000`, `libscf 3000`.
